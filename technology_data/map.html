MAP Core Facilities

The MAP core facility (Multiplexed Assay Platform) provides comprehensive support for multiplex biomarker analysis, from study design to data interpretation, serving basic, translational, and clinical research projects.

Services and main activities

The MAP facility offers a flexible and scalable approach through a wide range of kits, allowing the analysis of predefined or custom biomarker panels adapted to sample origin (human or murine) and type (cell culture supernatants, serum, plasma, biological fluids, and cell lysates). Data acquisition and analysis are supported by a secure, fast, and user-friendly software environment (DISCOVERY WORKBENCH 4.0), ensuring robust and reliable results.

Support is currently provided on a consulting basis, including guidance in experimental design, biomarker selection, and data interpretation. At present, no dedicated technical staff is assigned to the platform, and analyses are performed in collaboration with project teams.

Experimental Design & Consulting

  • Selection of appropriate biomarker panels and technologies
  • Study design optimization (sample type, cohort size, controls)
  • Feasibility assessment and project planning

Sample Processing & Analysis

  • Processing of diverse biological samples (serum, plasma, cell culture supernatants, etc.)
  • Multiplex quantification of cytokines, chemokines, growth factors, and other soluble markers
  • High-throughput analysis using validated and standardized protocols

Quality Control

  • Monitoring of assay performance and reproducibility
  • Standardization of protocols across projects
  • Data validation and quality assessment

Data Analysis & Interpretation

  • Data processing, normalization, and visualization
  • Statistical analysis
  • Support for biological interpretation and biomarker signature identification

Translational & Clinical Applications

  • Biomarker discovery and validation
  • Patient stratification and subgroup identification
  • Monitoring of disease progression and therapeutic response
