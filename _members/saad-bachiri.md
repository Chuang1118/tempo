Saad Bachiri has always been interested in biology, with a particular focus on immunology and cancer research. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Biology and is currently enrolled in the first year of a Master’s degree in Immunology, Infectious Diseases and Immunotherapy at Université Paris-Est Créteil.

As part of his Master’s program, Saad is conducting a research internship at Inserm under the supervision of Kevin Saitoski, where he works on a project exploring the role of lipoprotein metabolism in chronic lymphocytic leukemia.

Through this experience, he is developing skills in cellular and molecular biology and gaining a deeper understanding of cancer biology. He is particularly interested in the links between metabolism and disease.
