Master’s Program in Inflammation & Inflammatory Diseases (IMI)
<!-- excerpt end -->

The Inflammation and Inflammatory Diseases program aims to deepen knowledge in the field of inflammation and its involvement in various human pathologies. This program offers a transdisciplinary approach to the study of inflammatory processes at the interface between fundamental and applied/clinical research. This training is supported by the [Labex Inflamex](http://inflamex.fr/).
